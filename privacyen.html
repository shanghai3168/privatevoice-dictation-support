Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

1. Overview

PrivateVoice Dictation is a fully offline voice dictation application for macOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. All speech processing happens entirely on your device.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Voice recordings or audio data
  • Transcribed text
  • Personal information
  • Usage data
  • Any other user-generated content

3. Local Processing

All speech recognition is performed locally using on-device AI models (SenseVoice, Qwen3-ASR, Moonshine, Parakeet). No data is sent to our servers or any third-party servers.

4. User Lexicon (CSV)

Your custom lexicon data is stored only on your Mac. Exporting and importing CSV files is done entirely locally.

5. Permissions

The app requests Microphone, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring permissions solely to enable voice input functionality. These permissions are not used for any data collection.

6. Open Source

This app is a secondary development based on the open-source project VoiceSnap under the MIT License.
